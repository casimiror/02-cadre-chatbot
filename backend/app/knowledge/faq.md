## How to get started with Cadre
Send a message to talk to an AI Strategist: through the contact form on the website, by email to
hello@gocadre.ai, or ask this assistant to send the request for you. If you want a baseline first,
the AI Maturity Index scores your company across Cadre's eight-pillar framework. Most engagements
then start with the 45-day AI Transformation Intensive.

## Booking a call or meeting with an AI strategist
Cadre does not use a self-serve calendar. To book or schedule a call or meeting with an AI
strategist, fill in the contact form (first name, last name, company, work email, optional phone,
and how Cadre can help) or email hello@gocadre.ai; the team replies to schedule the call. This assistant can also send the request
for you if you share those details in the chat. Office hours are Monday to Friday, 8:00 AM to
6:00 PM Pacific.

## Pricing and cost
Pricing depends on scope and is discussed on a strategy call. The assistant does not quote prices,
discounts or timelines beyond the published program lengths (45-day Intensive, 60-day data build).

## Why work with a partner instead of doing AI in-house
According to MIT, over 90% of AI initiatives fail, often because internal teams lack the time,
experience or structure to lead AI work full time. Companies that bring in a dedicated AI partner
are about three times more likely to succeed. Cadre is that partner, and it also builds internal
capability (AI champions, training) so the client is not dependent on Cadre forever.

## Can Cadre work with our current tools and systems
Yes. Cadre starts with a deep review of the current tech stack and processes, then identifies
where AI can plug in, from simple workflow automation to agents that connect several tools for
fully automated execution. It activates AI features in tools the client already pays for before
recommending anything new.

## What types of companies are the best fit
Companies of all sizes, especially those with manual workflows that get less efficient as they
grow. B2B and B2C services companies often fit, as do private equity backed companies that want to
grow efficiently and expand EBITDA without ballooning headcount.

## Client portal access
Cadre gives clients a centralized portal to track their AI tools, agents, training and results,
so teams stay aligned and accountable and can scale what works. Access is set up by the client's
Cadre team during the engagement. Existing clients who cannot log in, or need access for a new
colleague, should contact their Cadre team or hello@gocadre.ai; the assistant cannot reset
passwords or see account data, so it escalates account problems to a person.

## NDAs, confidentiality and client security requirements
Cadre routinely works under confidentiality agreements and published case studies never name the
client without permission. Specific contract terms, security questionnaires and data processing
agreements are handled by the Cadre team, not by this assistant: ask for a strategy call or
escalate.
