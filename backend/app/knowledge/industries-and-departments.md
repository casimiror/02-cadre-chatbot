## Industries Cadre works with
Cadre works with companies of all sizes, and is especially valuable to businesses with manual
workflows that get less efficient as they grow; B2B and B2C services companies often fit, as do
private equity backed companies that want to grow EBITDA without growing headcount. Industries with
dedicated expertise: professional services, private equity, real estate, financial services,
mortgage & lending, construction, retail & e-commerce, manufacturing & logistics and hospitality.
Cadre has also worked with companies "you wouldn't expect to use AI": if an industry is not listed,
a strategy call is the way to explore what is possible.

## Professional services
AI that automates delivery, accelerates timelines and lets experts focus on the work only they can
do, turning billable hours into scalable profit.

## Private equity
AI built for private equity operations: faster deal flow, compressed due diligence, and more value
from portfolio companies, helping PE-backed companies expand EBITDA without ballooning headcount.

## Real estate
AI that automates property analysis, qualifies leads instantly and keeps the pipeline moving.

## Financial services, mortgage and lending
Financial services: faster client onboarding, automated compliance and personalized advice.
Mortgage & lending: AI that automates underwriting and accelerates approvals to close loans faster
and protect margins.

## Construction
AI that automates takeoffs, tracks project health and keeps jobs on schedule and budget. Cadre
publishes construction-specific work on job cost reports, estimating from completed jobs, bid
leveling with subcontractor history and labor burden, plus the State of AI in Construction 2026
whitepaper.

## Retail, e-commerce, manufacturing, logistics and hospitality
Retail & e-commerce: personalization, fewer returns and higher lifetime value. Manufacturing &
logistics: predict problems before downtime, optimize inventory, accelerate fulfillment.
Hospitality: personalized guest service, demand prediction and efficient property operations.

## Departments Cadre improves
Cadre goes department by department, and tailors work to each team's workflows, fears and
opportunities:
- Sales: automated prospecting, instant lead qualification, less manual pipeline work.
- Marketing: campaign generation, faster testing, content at scale, proven ROI.
- Customer success: predict at-risk accounts, automate health monitoring, personalized engagement.
- Finance: faster close, automated reconciliation, accurate forecasting.
- Operations: automated workflows, fewer bottlenecks, less manual intervention.
- Executive leadership: intelligence synthesized across the business without waiting for reports.
- Technology: automated code review, faster incident response, less technical debt.
- Legal: faster contract review, automated legal research, instant risk flags.
