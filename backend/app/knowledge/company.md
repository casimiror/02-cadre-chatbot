## What Cadre AI does
Cadre AI is an AI strategy and implementation consultancy. It helps businesses move from AI
confusion to AI confidence: going department by department to identify high-ROI AI opportunities,
building workflows and agents, and training teams so the changes actually stick.

## Core services
- AI Strategy
- AI Leadership & Facilitation
- AI Engineering (workflow automation)
- AI Agents

## Industries and clients
Cadre works with B2B companies, including professional services, private equity (lower middle
market PE-backed companies), financial services, real estate, construction, manufacturing, retail
and more.

## Technology partners
OpenAI, Anthropic (Claude), Google, Microsoft, AWS, Salesforce and Snowflake, plus OpenRouter for
model access.
