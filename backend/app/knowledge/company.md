## What Cadre AI does
Cadre AI is an AI strategy and implementation firm. It takes companies "from AI confusion to AI
confidence": it pinpoints the right AI opportunities, implements them, and measures the business
impact. Cadre is a consultancy focused on using AI to drive revenue growth and improve EBITDA.
Many companies get less efficient as they scale; Cadre helps them scale with less overhead by
finding the right AI strategy instead of throwing tools at the problem.

Cadre's promise to clients has three parts:
- Drive revenue: scale faster with AI-driven automation and predictive insights.
- Increase profitability: optimize operations and remove inefficiencies to grow margins and
  enterprise value.
- Elevate employees: remove tedious tasks so teams focus on high-impact, meaningful work.

## Find, Prepare, Implement: Cadre's approach
Real AI transformation requires finding the right problems, preparing people to lead the change,
and implementing solutions that drive measurable results. Cadre works department by department to
identify and implement AI workflows and agents with real ROI, helps select and configure the LLM(s)
that fit the client's tech stack and goals, and teaches teams how to use AI effectively, when to
rely on it, and when to lead without it.

## Services overview
Cadre offers four core services: AI Strategy, AI Leadership & Facilitation, AI Engineering and AI
Agents. Engagements usually start with the AI Transformation Intensive (part of AI Strategy).
Cadre also offers a Data & Dashboards build and Next Generation Education for students.

## Track record
Cadre has delivered more than 100 high-ROI AI use cases across more than 50 companies. Cadre cites
MIT's State of AI in Business 2025: AI projects developed with specialized vendors succeed about
three times more often than in-house projects.

## Technology partners
Cadre is an official Anthropic and OpenAI partner, and was one of the first official OpenAI
Service Partners (announced January 2026), helping companies roll out ChatGPT for Business, build
CustomGPTs and AI agents, and train their teams. Cadre also works with tools from Google,
Microsoft (including Copilot), Salesforce and Snowflake, and recommends the platform that fits each
client instead of a single vendor.
