## What the AI Maturity Index is
The AI Maturity Index scores a company across Cadre's eight-pillar framework for AI transformation.
The company gets a grade in each pillar with a clear explanation, plus actionable insights on how
to improve and move further along its AI journey. It is the second stage of the AI Transformation
Intensive, and the 12-month roadmap is designed to raise the score over time, so the Index works
both as a baseline and as the measure of progress.

## The eight pillars of AI transformation
1. Dedicated AI team: one fully accountable AI leader, supported by product, strategy, research and
   engineering, working across departments under the CEO.
2. AI command center: one company-wide AI platform (for example ChatGPT, Copilot or Claude) so
   employees stop using personal accounts with company data, with features like projects and
   custom GPTs actually used.
3. AI-first culture: clear AI policies, CEO communication that addresses employee fears, and change
   management with deep user involvement.
4. Connected tech stack: API access on existing systems (CRM, ERP, etc.) so agents can push and
   pull data, and an audit of built-in AI features before buying anything new.
5. AI-healthy data: clean, structured data and knowing where it is created, transformed and stored.
6. AI agent readiness: documented SOPs, infrastructure access and company templates so agents can
   work like digital employees, with monitoring, feedback and training.
7. Departmental AI deep dives: each team's people, processes and technology assessed for AI,
   agentic workflows or traditional automation.
8. Three-year AI vision: the company's dream state as the most scalable AI-enabled organization in
   its category, used as a benchmark like an annual budget.

## Maturity levels
Each pillar is graded on five levels, and the overall Index summarizes them:
- Level 1, Exploring: individual, unsanctioned use of AI tools; no policy, owner or budget.
- Level 2, Experimenting: a few pilots and licenses in some teams; results are anecdotal and data
  is siloed.
- Level 3, Operationalizing: an accountable AI owner, an approved platform, an AI policy, and the
  first workflows in production with measured ROI.
- Level 4, Scaling: AI across several departments, connected systems and modeled data, agents with
  human oversight, governance and monitoring in place.
- Level 5, AI-native: AI is part of how every department plans and works; new use cases go from
  idea to production through a repeatable process and the roadmap is reviewed continuously.
Most companies Cadre meets start between levels 1 and 2 in most pillars.

## How a company gets scored
The assessment combines a survey sent to leaders and employees across departments with interviews
by Cadre strategists. It captures how AI is used today, which tools are licensed, where data lives,
how decisions about AI are made, and the team's areas of excitement and fear and preferred ways of
learning. Cadre then grades each pillar against the five levels and reviews the results with the
leadership team. To get scored, request it through a strategy call (contact form, email or this
assistant); the Index is included in the AI Transformation Intensive.

## What the AI Maturity Index report contains
- A grade per pillar with the evidence behind it.
- An overall maturity level and the gaps that hold it back.
- Risks found (for example company data in personal AI accounts, no AI policy, unowned pilots).
- Quick wins that can move a pillar up within weeks.
- Recommendations that feed the use case library and the 12-month roadmap.
Cadre recommends re-running the Index about every 6 to 12 months to measure progress.
