## Case studies overview
All published case studies are with non-disclosed companies. Results at a glance:
- 45 hours saved monthly: AI lead processing agent (professional services).
- 220 hours saved monthly: AI supplier automation (manufacturing & logistics).
- 8,000+ hours saved annually: AI proposal automation (manufacturing & logistics).
- About 420,000 USD saved annually: AI-powered housing visibility system (hospitality).
- 57% increase in daily efficiency: AI scheduling system (real estate).
- 3,500 hours saved annually: AI email agent (manufacturing & logistics).
- 2,500 hours saved annually: Loan Intelligence Assistant (financial services, mortgage & lending).
- 1,500 hours saved annually: AI voice and chat agents (professional services).

## AI lead processing agent (professional services)
A sales coordinator manually monitored a shared inbox across five branches, spending 3-5 minutes
per lead on CRM entry and 30-45 minutes each morning importing and assigning leads. Cadre built an
automation that monitors the inbox 24/7, classifies emails, checks the CRM for existing customers,
creates cards with AI notes and assigns leads by real-time availability and branch rules. Result:
1,500+ emails and 650+ leads handled monthly across 5 branches, 45 hours saved per month.

## AI supplier automation (manufacturing & logistics)
Staff processed 200-300 supplier confirmation emails a day, cross-referencing Zendesk and NetSuite
at 2-6 minutes each. Cadre's automation extracts and matches supplier data against NetSuite and
confirms orders automatically when they match, flagging exceptions for human review. Result:
processing time cut by more than half, 220 hours saved monthly.

## AI proposal automation (manufacturing & logistics)
Proposals took 1-2 days per option because of manual fixture values, rebate calculations, part ID
lookups and price guides. Cadre standardized part IDs for automatic lookup and auto-estimated rebates
by location, leaving about 10% of the work for human review. Result: 20-30 proposals a week
processed in about 20 minutes each, 8,000+ hours saved annually.

## AI housing visibility system (hospitality)
An outdated booking system without occupancy visibility caused same-day "flip day" turnovers that
required expedited cleaning. Cadre built an AI booking visibility dashboard integrated with the CRM
that blocks conflicting same-day bookings. Result: about 420,000 USD saved annually.

## AI field scheduling system (real estate)
Field specialists scheduled inspections from spreadsheets and drove inefficient routes. Cadre built
an AI field scheduling platform with route optimization, territory assignment, 25+ prioritization
rules and 6+ platform integrations, scheduling two weeks ahead. Result: 57% increase in daily
efficiency, 50% more inspection capacity and 72% lower fuel expenses.

## AI email agent (manufacturing & logistics)
55 sales reps sorted and drafted replies to high-volume email by hand. Cadre deployed an email agent
with call recording that organizes inboxes and drafts responses from historical patterns. Result:
4,000+ emails a month processed and drafted, email work down from 9 hours to about 55 minutes per
week per person, 3,500 hours saved annually.

## Loan Intelligence Assistant (mortgage & lending)
Loan officers navigated complex guidelines across disconnected systems, taking 1-2 days per loan.
Cadre built a custom AI assistant that unified loan tools: guideline lookup, investor matching and
borrower communication in one place. Result: 27 loan officers using it daily, 3,960+ inquiries in
the first 90 days, 2,500 hours saved annually.

## AI voice and chat agents (professional services)
Consultation requests by phone and SMS were booked by hand. Cadre deployed voice and chat agents
integrated with the scheduling software for instant availability checks and automatic booking.
Result: 500-700 appointment requests a month confirmed immediately, 24/7, 1,500 hours saved
annually.
