## AI Strategy
AI Strategy is for mid-market and enterprise leadership teams that know they need AI but not where
to start. It begins with the AI Transformation Intensive. Cadre interviews teams across
departments to find real problems, calculates the potential ROI of each opportunity and ranks them
by impact and feasibility. The result is quick wins with measurable EBITDA impact, a 3-year roadmap
built on proven wins, and help implementing it, not a slide deck left behind.

The approach has four steps:
1. Discover use cases: interview departments, estimate ROI, prioritize by impact and feasibility.
2. Survey the landscape: research existing tools, track AI capabilities launching in the next 3-6
   months, identify gaps where current solutions fall short.
3. Implement solutions: deploy the chosen tools, integrate them into daily workflows, train the
   team, monitor usage and iterate.
4. Scale with confidence: expand to other departments, establish internal AI champions, build the
   3-year roadmap.

## AI Transformation Intensive (45 days)
The AI Transformation Intensive is a structured 45-day engagement that goes from kickoff to a
12-month roadmap. It is a co-authored blueprint, written by the client's leadership team and
Cadre's, for how the organization will evolve with AI over the next 3 years. Its six stages:
1. Kickoff: both leadership teams agree deliverables and timelines.
2. AI Maturity Index: maps where the organization stands, areas of excitement and fear, preferred
   learning styles and current AI usage.
3. Full-day workshop: with executive sponsors, go-to-market, revops, delivery, operations, support
   and finance, to find ways AI can drive revenue, profitability and employee impact.
4. Use case library: AI use cases sorted into immediate fits, future projects and low-value ideas.
5. Three-year vision: which roles stay human-led, which are automated, and how daily work changes.
6. Twelve-month roadmap: prioritized initiatives and deep dives, paced to what the organization can
   absorb and designed to raise its AI Maturity Index score.

## AI Leadership & Facilitation
AI Leadership & Facilitation prepares executives and teams before AI tools can pay off: "you can
spend millions on AI tools and get zero ROI if your people aren't ready." Sessions are experiential,
not lectures, and combine technical implementation, behavioral science and executive coaching:
- 30% teaching: best practices from real implementations, the Eight Pillars framework, spotting
  AI-ready use cases, messaging AI adoption to skeptical teams.
- 30% interaction: live practice on real resistance scenarios (skeptical department heads,
  employees worried about their jobs, competing priorities).
- 40% application: work on the client's own problems; leaders leave with 3 to 5 prioritized AI
  opportunities, quick wins versus long-term plays, and a next-step action plan.
Session formats: 2-day leadership intensive, 1-day workshop, half-day executive session and 1-hour
virtual kickoff. Sessions are tailored by department.

## AI Engineering
AI Engineering is Cadre's build service for teams losing time to manual work and disconnected
systems. Cadre starts with the business problem, then chooses between buying an existing tool,
automating the workflow across the systems the client already has, or building a custom AI agent
for complex, repetitive multi-step processes. Typical targets are data entry, document routing,
email triage, report generation and customer inquiry handling. Work includes connecting APIs across
the tech stack, adding AI that understands context and unstructured data, and designing workflows
that improve as AI capabilities grow. Cadre's rule of thumb: sometimes a low-cost off-the-shelf tool
plus smart automation solves in a week what a custom build would take months to do; Cadre only
builds custom when there is no faster or smarter option.

## AI Agents
AI Agents is Cadre's service for teams that want AI doing real work inside their workflows. Three
levels:
- Prompts & assistants: high-impact prompts and assistants for daily work and quick time savings.
- Voice agents: handle intake, qualification, support and internal routing on calls, turning calls
  into clean data and next steps.
- Fully fledged AI agents: plan, take actions across tools and run end-to-end processes with
  guardrails and human oversight.
Every agent is built for a specific team and workflow. Cadre's Agent Library includes agents such
as a 30-day social media calendar creator, access request provisioner, persistent customer
knowledge base, ad copy tester, AI adoption coach, audit & compliance tracker, auto-scheduler,
automated takeoff generator (construction) and automated underwriter (lending).

## Data & Dashboards (60-day data build)
Cadre connects a company's systems and models the data before building AI on it. The build takes
60 days (days 60 to 120 of an engagement, after the AI strategy is set). Cadre connects CRM, ERP,
accounting, HR/payroll and operational systems plus recurring spreadsheets and feeds into one data
warehouse, normalizes the data, writes agreed definitions into a semantic layer (so "gross margin"
means one thing everywhere), and builds a data model per use case, with 3 to 5 use cases live in the
60 days. Data refreshes nightly, more often on the enterprise tier. Documents (contracts, SOPs,
reports, email threads) are a separate build scoped by a solution architect, using a knowledge graph
or a direct document connection. Clients can keep Power BI or Tableau pointed at the new warehouse.

## Next Generation Education
Next Generation Education teaches high school and college students to build an AI career edge with
the same training approach Cadre uses with enterprise clients: AI fluency fundamentals, portfolio
building, applied career skills (research, writing, coding, analysis), and AI-informed resume
building and job searching. Interested students or schools can register interest through the
website contact form.

## Buy, automate or build
Cadre's tools philosophy: sometimes Cadre builds, sometimes it does not. If a tool the client
already uses has AI features, Cadre helps activate them. If a good off-the-shelf tool exists, Cadre
recommends the best fit. Cadre builds custom solutions only when there is no faster or smarter
option. Cadre starts every engagement with a review of the current tech stack and processes, so it
works with the client's existing tools and systems.
